Questions

Before you buy

Do I need Excel?

No. A free Google account is enough. You get a copy link; the sheet opens in Google Sheets on phone or computer. To use Microsoft Excel instead, download your copy from File › Download › Microsoft Excel.

Does it send WhatsApp messages automatically?

No. It prepares each message and opens WhatsApp with the text filled in. You choose who to remind and press send yourself. This keeps you within WhatsApp's rules and in control of what parents receive.

Can parents pay through the sheet?

No. The reminder includes your UPI ID so parents can pay you directly. You then add the payment as one row.

I'm not good with spreadsheets.

You only type in the yellow cells: names, numbers, dates. Everything grey is calculated. The Start Here tab walks through setup, and sample rows show exactly what to enter.
