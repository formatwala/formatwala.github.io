Refund and Cancellation Policy

Format Wala · Last updated: 5 November 2026

What you are buying

Format Wala sells digital products: ready-made spreadsheet templates for Google Sheets and Microsoft Excel. Access is delivered immediately after successful payment: you are taken to a Google Sheets "Make a copy" page, which saves your own copy to your Google Drive. You can also download it as an Excel file from File › Download. If you miss the page, email us with your payment ID and we will resend the link.

2-day fix-or-refund promise

If the product does not work as described on its sales page, email us within 2 days of purchase at formatwala.support@gmail.com with:

  • your name and the email used for payment,
  • the Razorpay payment ID (from your receipt), and
  • a short description or screenshot of the problem.

We will first try to fix the problem. If we cannot fix it within 7 days of your email, we will give a full refund.

When refunds are not given

Because a digital file cannot be returned once delivered, we do not refund:

  • a change of mind after the product has been delivered,
  • problems caused by editing or deleting the template's formulas,
  • requests made more than 2 days after purchase,
  • features the sales page does not claim (for example: automatic WhatsApp sending, online fee collection inside the sheet, GST e-invoicing).

Duplicate or failed payments

If you were charged twice, or money left your account but you did not receive the product, email us. Duplicate charges are refunded in full. Failed payments are usually reversed automatically by your bank within 5–7 working days.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are made through Razorpay to the original payment method. Razorpay normally processes them within 5–7 working days; your bank may take longer to show the credit.

Cancellation

Orders are delivered instantly, so they cannot be cancelled after payment. See the fix-or-refund promise above.
