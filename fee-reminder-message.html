English fee reminder messages

Before the due date (polite)

Dear [Parent name], a gentle reminder that [Student name]'s tuition fee of ₹[amount] for [Month] is due by [date]. You can pay by UPI to [UPI ID]. Thank you! – [Centre name]

On the due date

Dear [Parent name], [Student name]'s fee of ₹[amount] for [Month] is due today. UPI: [UPI ID]. Please ignore this message if you have already paid. Thank you – [Centre name]

A few days overdue

Dear [Parent name], we have not yet received [Student name]'s fee of ₹[amount] for [Month]. Could you please pay this week? UPI: [UPI ID]. If there is any difficulty, just let me know. – [Centre name]

Part payment received, balance pending

Dear [Parent name], thank you for paying ₹[paid] towards [Student name]'s [Month] fee. The balance is ₹[balance]. UPI: [UPI ID]. – [Centre name]

Two months pending (firm but kind)

Dear [Parent name], [Student name]'s fees for [Month 1] and [Month 2] (₹[total]) are pending. Please clear them by [date] so classes can continue without interruption. Happy to discuss an instalment plan if needed. – [Centre name]

Thank-you after payment

Dear [Parent name], we have received ₹[amount] for [Student name]'s [Month] fee. Receipt no. [R-0012]. Thank you! – [Centre name]

Tips that get fees paid on time

  • Send one message per parent, not a list in the class group. Naming families in a group embarrasses them and rarely works.
  • Always include the exact amount and your UPI ID, so paying takes one step.
  • Send the first reminder 2 to 3 days before the due date. Most late fees are simply forgotten.
  • Thank parents when they pay and give a receipt number. It builds trust and avoids "I already paid" confusion.
  • Keep a record of who paid what and when. A simple register prevents disputes.
