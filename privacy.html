Privacy Policy

Format Wala · Last updated: 5 November 2026

This notice explains what personal data we collect when you buy from Format Wala, why, and your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Who we are

Format Wala is run by Madhan, Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. Contact: formatwala.support@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you buy a product we collect only:

  • your name,
  • your email address,
  • your phone number (if you choose to give it), and
  • payment details needed to confirm the order (payment ID, amount, date, status).

Card numbers, UPI PINs and bank logins are entered only on Razorpay's secure checkout. We never see or store them.

Why we collect it

  • To deliver the product you bought and send your receipt.
  • To answer your support questions and process refunds.
  • To send you free updates to products you bought.
  • To keep accounting and tax records, as the law requires.

We do not send promotional email unless you have agreed to receive it, and you can stop it at any time by replying "unsubscribe".

Who we share it with

  • Razorpay Software Pvt. Ltd., to process payments.
  • Google, because our email and files are hosted on Google Workspace / Gmail.
  • Government authorities, only when the law requires it.

We never sell or rent your personal data.

Your data inside the templates

Student, parent and fee details you type into a Format Wala template stay in your own Google Drive or computer. We cannot see them.

How long we keep it

Order and payment records are kept for 8 years for tax and accounting purposes. Support emails are kept for up to 2 years. Other data is deleted when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you,
  • correct or update it,
  • erase it (except records we must keep by law), or
  • withdraw consent to any promotional email.

Email formatwala.support@gmail.com. We will reply within 7 working days.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
